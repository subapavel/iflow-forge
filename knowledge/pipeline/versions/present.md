## How to read

A `present in` row: the artifact exists in that package download and the package's CHANGELOG.txt has no entry that adds it. The version gate treats it as present for tenants at that version or newer and unknown below, and raises no question for it (D-038). The date is that of the first CHANGELOG line the row cites.
