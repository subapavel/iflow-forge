# mapReceiverAliasToBusinessSystemName

## Purpose

Translates header SAP_ReceiverAlias into the receiver's business system name through the Partner Directory alternative partner of agency tenantStage (else "PRD") and scheme BusinessSystemName, and sets header SAP_Receiver; a party prefix "Party.Alias" is split off [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/mapReceiverAliasToBusinessSystemName.groovy#L13] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/mapReceiverAliasToBusinessSystemName.groovy#L18] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/mapReceiverAliasToBusinessSystemName.groovy#L32] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/mapReceiverAliasToBusinessSystemName.groovy#L33] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/mapReceiverAliasToBusinessSystemName.groovy#L36] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/mapReceiverAliasToBusinessSystemName.groovy#L55].
Script step "Map to Business Name" in the main process of Step01 XI, Step02 inbound, Step02 integrated messaging async and sync, and Step04, once the receiver alias is known (Used by). On the virtual-stage paths tenantStage is set before it in one of three ways: one receiver stage, by mapSenderStageToReceiverStage [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.async, scripts/mapSenderStageToReceiverStage.groovy#L251]; several stages, per split message by the Content Modifier "Set Tenant Stage" from `/Receiver/Service` [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.async, step 79 "Set Tenant Stage"] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.async, step 91 "Set Tenant Stage"] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step04.receiver.determination, step 64 "Set Tenant Stage"]; no receiver stage, set to "PRD" only for an unknown stage, otherwise not written [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.async, scripts/mapSenderStageToReceiverStage.groovy#L239] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.async, scripts/mapSenderStageToReceiverStage.groovy#L240] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.async, scripts/mapSenderStageToReceiverStage.groovy#L241] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.async, scripts/mapSenderStageToReceiverStage.groovy#L247].
