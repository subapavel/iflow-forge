# readCustomXFromPD

## Purpose

Decides whether the scenario-specific custom exit runs in the current pipeline step: reads `<CustomXType>Enabled`, a `|`-separated list of step numbers, and `<CustomXType>Endpoint` from headers or the Partner Directory and sets properties CustomXBoolean and CustomXEndpoint [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L38] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L45] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L53] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L58] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L59].
Script step "Check scenario-specific exit" in the local process "Call custom exits (global and scenario-specific)" of eight generic iFlows, before the router "Scenario-specific exit?"; each sets CustomXType to CustomXPre beforehand, so the keys read are CustomXPreEnabled and CustomXPreEndpoint (Used by).

## Gotchas

- Precedence per setting: header before Partner Directory [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L38] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L40] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L45] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L47]; Enabled and Endpoint are resolved independently, so a header Enabled can pair with a Partner Directory Endpoint.
- One of the two missing: neither property is set (no CustomXBoolean 'false') [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L52].
- The match is exact string equality, so "1" does not match the step "01" [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L54].
- Without "generic.step" in `${camelId}`, `indexOf` returns -1 and the step becomes the characters at 0-based positions 11 and 12 of camelId (`substring(11, 13)`) [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L28] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L29].
- A camelId too short for `substring(index, index + 2)` (for example under 13 characters without "generic.step") throws StringIndexOutOfBoundsException [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L29].
- Without property CustomXType the keys become "nullEnabled" and "nullEndpoint" (Groovy string concatenation with null) [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L35] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L38] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L41].
- Lines 73 to 84 are a commented-out older logger [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.inbound.processing, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L73].
- The global exit is not read here: "Initialize exit" sets CustomXGlobalBoolean and CustomXGlobalEndpoint from the properties `<CustomXType>Global_Enabled` and `_Endpoint`, and the router "Global exit?" follows this script, for example in Step01 IDoc [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step01.inbound.processing.idoc, step 7 "Initialize exit"] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step01.inbound.processing.idoc, step 9 "Global exit?"].
- The integrated runtimes determine receivers and interfaces in their own local process, so their exits see step 02, never 04 or 05 [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.async, process "Determine receivers & interfaces"] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.sync, process "Determine receivers & interfaces"] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.async, scripts/readCustomXFromPD.groovy#L28].
