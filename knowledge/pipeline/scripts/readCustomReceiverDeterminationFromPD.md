# readCustomReceiverDeterminationFromPD

## Purpose

Chooses between a custom receiver determination flow and a reused extended receiver determination (XRD) flow: the endpoint comes from header customXRDEndpoint or Partner Directory CustomXRDEndpoint, else from ReuseXRDEndpoint, and lands in property customXRDEndpoint with the flags customXRDBoolean and reuseXRDBoolean [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.async, scripts/readCustomReceiverDeterminationFromPD.groovy#L21] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.async, scripts/readCustomReceiverDeterminationFromPD.groovy#L25] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.async, scripts/readCustomReceiverDeterminationFromPD.groovy#L35] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.async, scripts/readCustomReceiverDeterminationFromPD.groovy#L43].
Script step "Read custom receiver determination" before the router "Custom RD?" in Step04 and in the local process "Determine receivers & interfaces" of both integrated runtimes [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step04.receiver.determination, step 32 "Custom RD?"] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.async, step 47 "Custom RD?"] [com.sap.integration.cloud.pipeline.v2.generic.step02.integrated.messaging.sync, step 33 "Custom RD?"].
