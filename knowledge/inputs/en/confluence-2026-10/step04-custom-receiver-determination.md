# Step04 – Custom Receiver Determination

An empty skeleton (only the ProcessDirect entry + Exception Handling) — you add the whole receiver determination logic yourself. The output must be a Receivers XML.

> Integrated runtime: usable, but the output must then contain both receivers and interfaces (Receivers with nested Interfaces) — in the integrated runtime interfaces are not determined separately.
